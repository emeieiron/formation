# Mosaic

![Six phones forming the Solana mark in a finished game of Mosaic](images/mosaic.png)

**Players:** 6 or 9 · **Goal:** seal every seam before time runs out · **Wrong pairs allowed:** 2

## Setup

- Each phone shows one piece of the Solana mark, at the same physical size on every phone.
- Lay the phones in three rows, one row per bar of the mark: 2 phones per row for 6 players, 3 for 9.
- Move phones until every bar lines up across them.

## Actions

- A seam is the edge between two neighbouring phones: 7 seams with 6 phones, 12 with 9.
- To seal a seam, both players pinch across it: each swipes toward the shared edge, and both let go at the same moment (within 0.2 s), at the same spot along the edge.

## Rules

- A pinch whose two halves don't line up along the edge doesn't seal. Try again; it costs nothing.
- Pinching with a phone that isn't your neighbour is a wrong pair. The third wrong pair ends the attempt.
- The attempt ends if any seam is still open when the clock runs out.
- Every phone's screen must be large enough to show a piece; smaller phones can't join.

## Difficulty

| Setting | Time | Pinch tolerance | Help |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Easy | 20 s + 9 s per seam | 8 mm | Each piece shows its place in the mark; open seams are marked |
| Normal | 20 s + 7 s per seam | 8 mm | Open seams are marked |
| Hard | 20 s + 5 s per seam | 6 mm | |
| Extreme | 20 s + 4 s per seam | 5 mm | |
