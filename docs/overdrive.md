# Overdrive

![Two phones mid-game in Overdrive](images/overdrive.png)

**Players:** 2 · **Goal:** clear 12 waves within 28 seconds · **Misses allowed:** none

## Setup

- Each phone shows a square with four symbols on its edges.
- Beside your partner's name, your phone shows the symbol **they** need. You can't see your own.

## Each wave

1. Call out the symbol your phone shows for your partner.
2. Tap your square to rotate it one step clockwise.
3. A pulse falls toward each square. When it lands, the symbol at the top of the square must be the one your partner called.
4. The wave clears when both players catch.

## Rules

- A wrong symbol at the top when the pulse lands is a miss. One miss ends the attempt.
- The attempt also ends if 12 waves aren't cleared before the clock runs out.
- Every cleared wave makes both pulses fall 8% faster.
- In waves 1–4 the two pulses land one after the other; from wave 5 they land together.
- From wave 9 you also see your partner's next symbol.
- There is no difficulty setting.
