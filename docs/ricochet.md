# Ricochet

![Two phones forming one Ricochet arena](images/ricochet.png)

**Players:** 2 · **Goal:** clear 6 targets before time runs out · **Misses allowed:** 2

## Setup

- Place the two phones side by side. Each shows half of one arena, with your paddle on the outer edge.

## Actions

- Tap or drag up and down to move your paddle.
- Catch the pulse with your paddle to send it back across the arena.
- Where it meets the paddle sets the angle: the centre returns it straight, an edge sends it off at up to 55°.

## Rules

- A pulse that hits a target clears it.
- A pulse that gets past your paddle is a miss. The third shared miss ends the attempt.
- Each return by the other player after yours speeds the pulse up 12%, to at most 1.9×.
- Two such exchanges charge the next return: it breaks through one target and keeps going.
- The attempt ends if 6 targets aren't cleared before the clock runs out.
