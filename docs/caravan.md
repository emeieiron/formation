# Caravan

![Two phones mid-walk in Caravan: the leader on the left pacing ahead, a lagging squad member on the right prompted to pick up pace](images/caravan.png)

**Players:** 6–16 · **Goal:** every player reaches 15,000 steps within 3 hours · **Pack spread limit:** 30 steps

## Setup

- Gather a squad of 6 to 16 players in the same physical space.
- Put the phones on the host's Wi-Fi network, or connect directly to the Seeker's local network hotspot opened from the lobby.
- Everyone holds their phone in hand or in a pocket as the squad walks together.

## The walk

1. When the countdown ends, the squad sets off together.
2. Each phone counts strides from its owner's movement. **Tap to Step** also adds a step and follows the same pacing rules.
3. The shared progress follows the player with the fewest steps. The squad wins only when **every player** reaches 15,000; steps are not pooled.
4. Your screen shows your personal count, the remaining time, your squad status, and each walker's count.

## Rules

- All players must reach **15,000 steps each** before the 3-hour timer expires. There is no difficulty setting.
- **The pack rule:** you can advance up to 30 steps ahead of the slowest player. At that limit, **WAIT FOR SQUAD** appears with a vibration. Further steps do not count until the others catch up.
- **Pacing alerts:** the player or players at the rear see **PICK UP PACE** when the leader is at least four steps ahead. The leader is marked as leading when the spread exceeds two steps.
- Very rapid repeated movements or taps do not add extra steps. Keep a steady walking pace; the counter does not measure physical distance between players.
- Reaching your own goal does not finish the group attempt. Stay connected while the remaining players finish.
- Keep every phone connected over the local network. A disconnection stops accepted steps. Reconnecting ends the interrupted round, and the group can try again with the same squad.
- If time expires before everyone reaches the goal, the attempt ends without a win.

## After the walk

A completed walk opens the existing Formation result. The Seeker unlocks the funded reward, and participants receive their shares through the same reward flow as the other games. A failed attempt offers another try.
